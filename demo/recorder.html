Recording steps

  1. Choose the StormSignal tab in the browser picker.
  2. Leave the capture running while the language, warning, safety guidance, and contacts appear.
  3. When the narration ends, the video is ready to save as stormsignal-demo.webm.

The exact IMD source date remains visible in the app. If the warning changes or the source is stale, the recording shows the current app state and says to verify the official page.
