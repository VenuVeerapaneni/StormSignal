ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Clear information. Clear source.

StormSignal reads the public IMD Chennai district-warning page and displays the official wording, source update date, and general safety steps. It does not provide street-level flood status, evacuation routes, or a live rescue service. Open the IMD source and follow local authority instructions.
