ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Clear information. Clear source.

StormSignal covers India by opening IMD’s public district-wise warning map. The map provides the official warning and forecast date for the district selected there. This prototype does not provide automatic location tracking, street-level conditions, shelters, evacuation routes, or rescue dispatch.
